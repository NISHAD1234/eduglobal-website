Eligibility for MBBS in India

  • Qualified NEET-UG in the same admission year, with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB) in 10+2.
  • Minimum qualifying percentile: 50th percentile for General category, 40th percentile for SC/ST/OBC, 45th percentile for General-PwD (as per the current NTA notification — confirm the exact cutoff for the applicable year on neet.nta.nic.in).
  • Minimum age: 17 years as of admission; upper age limit and other eligibility details are notified by NMC/state counselling authorities each year.

⚠️ NEET cutoffs, reservation percentages and eligibility rules are revised every year — always cross-check the current notification from NTA / MCC / your state's CET cell before advising students.

Documents Required for Admission

  • NEET-UG admit card
  • NEET-UG rank / scorecard
  • Online application form (state counselling portal)
  • 10th (SSC) marksheet & certificate
  • 12th (HSC) marksheet & certificate
  • Nationality certificate / passport
  • Domicile certificate
  • Aadhaar card
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Caste / caste-validity certificate (if applicable)
  • Non-creamy-layer certificate (if applicable, OBC)
  • PwD certificate from an authorized medical board (if applicable)
  • Category-specific certificates as required by your state (EWS, defence quota, minority quota, etc.)

Admission Process

  • All India Quota (15%) of Government college seats, plus 100% seats in Central/Deemed universities, are filled by MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) under DGHS.
  • State Quota (85%) of Government seats, plus Private college seats, are filled through your respective state counselling authority (e.g. DMER, state CET cell).
  • Counselling runs in multiple rounds (Round 1, Round 2, Mop-up/Stray Vacancy) — seat allotment is based on NEET rank, category, and choices filled.

What We Offer

Gomed Services

End-to-end support for your MBBS abroad journey — from your first NEET question to settling into campus.

Free Online NEET Crash Course (Limited Period Offer)

Gomed runs a free online NEET crash course to help students and parents map out the right path after NEET. Many students want MBBS in Russia, but they also want clarity on PG options in the USA. Our counselors walk you through the full journey step by step — NMC-approved universities, English-medium MBBS, total fees, hostel life, and visa planning — plus how to prepare early for USMLE, FMGE and other licensing exams, so you can choose with confidence before committing to an admission.

Career Planning & University Selection

Our counselors help you choose the right medical university for your MBBS abroad journey — not just the cheapest one. We check NMC approval, English-medium status, hospital training quality, support available for Indian students, hostel safety, and the real fee breakdown. If a PG in the USA is part of your plan, that groundwork needs to start from year one — our team connects your Russia MBBS choice with USMLE-focused guidance and future PG options, so you pick a university that fits your goals, not just your budget.

Documentation

Our documentation team handles the paperwork side of your admission and visa process so you don't have to navigate it alone. From passport and academic records to admission letters, visa files and travel documents, we keep the process organized and easy to track. Getting documentation right matters even more for MBBS in Russia — Gomed keeps you and your parents updated at every stage, from application to final travel.

Forex, Visa & Travel

Foreign exchange, fee transfers, visa applications and flight bookings are handled end-to-end by our team, so the move to study MBBS in Russia stays smooth for you and your family. We guide you through fee payment timelines, visa requirements and a safe travel plan — plus practical advice on what to carry, how to reach your university, and what to expect on arrival. The goal is a stress-free, well-planned transition from India to campus.

Online Coaching

Gomed helps students start preparing for their future medical career early. Many students choosing MBBS in Russia are also thinking ahead to PG in the USA, FMGE, NEET PG or other global licensing exams — and that planning works best when it starts in year one. We offer USMLE-focused guidance alongside a clear study roadmap, helping you build strong fundamentals, manage your time and prepare in stages. We're not just an MBBS-abroad admissions agent — we work with students as a long-term career-planning partner.

Pre & Post Departure Support

We support students before and after they leave India — not just at admission. Before travel, you and your parents get full guidance on packing, documentation, airport procedures, money, food, hostel life and safety. After arrival, our team helps you settle into your university, hostel and new city, including local formalities and everyday essentials. We stay in touch throughout, so parents stay informed and students feel supported — from the first step of an English-medium MBBS journey to long-term PG planning.

🇷🇺 MBBS in Russia

Study MBBS in Russia

Russia has long been one of the most-chosen MBBS abroad destinations for Indian students, largely due to its government-run medical universities, affordable fees, and decades of experience teaching international students.

Quick Facts

Duration6 years (including internship, as per current Russian academic norms)
Medium of InstructionEnglish, with compulsory local-language classes alongside the medical curriculum
EligibilityClass 12 with 50% aggregate in PCB (40% for reserved categories); qualifying NEET score mandatory; minimum age 17 by December 31 of admission year

MBBS in Russia — At a Glance

📅

MBBS in Russia is a 6-year course, including clinical rotations.

💵

Tuition starts from around Ruble 1,97,000/year — MBBS under Ruble 5,00,000/year is available at many universities.

🔤

The medium of instruction is English, at universities offering a fully English-medium program.

📈

Russia hosts one of the largest Indian medical-student communities abroad — an estimated 20,000–30,000+ students currently enrolled.

🏛️

Students are admitted only to government universities — there are no private medical universities in Russia, so there's no donation/capitation fee.

🎧

Every student applying through Gomed Consultants gets on-campus assistance for the entire course duration.

✅

NEET-UG must be qualified to sit for FMGE/NExT in India — the result stays valid for 3 years for MBBS admission abroad.

📝

Every university holds its own entrance exam, but at most it's largely a formality — a few (e.g. Kazan Federal University) are genuinely competitive.

Typical Fee Range

Roughly Ruble 1,97,000 to Ruble 13,00,000 tuition per year depending on university (a couple of premium Moscow universities price in USD) — mid-range options commonly fall between Ruble 3,00,000–5,00,000/year. Fees can typically be paid yearly or half-yearly. All Russian medical universities open to Indian students are government/public institutions, so there's no donation or capitation fee for admission.

⚠️ Other costs beyond tuition — hostel, Indian mess/food, flights, yearly medical insurance, visa extension and biometrics — vary by university. See the full directory below for per-university tuition and hostel details.

Why Students Choose Russia

  • Government-university status — no capitation fees
  • Long track record teaching Indian students
  • Relatively low cost of living
  • Multiple university choices across different cities

Things to Consider

  • Cold climate in many regions
  • Some universities historically taught in a bilingual (English + Russian) format — verify full English-medium status before applying
  • A local licensing exam is required after graduation

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NEET compulsory?
Yes — a qualifying NEET-UG score is mandatory for Indian students to practice in India after graduating abroad.
Is Russia safe for Indian students?
Russia hosts a large, established community of Indian medical students. As with any destination, check current advisories before travel.
What's the total cost including hostel and food?
Varies by city and university — ask our counselors for a full cost breakdown for your shortlisted universities.
Can I get an education loan?
Yes, most nationalized and private Indian banks offer education loans for NMC-compliant universities abroad.

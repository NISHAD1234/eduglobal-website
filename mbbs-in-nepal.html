🇳🇵 MBBS in Nepal

Study MBBS in Nepal

Nepal offers an MBBS curriculum, duration and academic pattern nearly identical to India's, making the academic transition easier for Indian students — though at a higher price point than most other destinations.

Medium of InstructionEnglish (Nepali also taught)
EligibilityStandard PCB/NEET criteria; entrance exam/counselling process at most colleges

Generally on the higher end, with a significant portion of fees payable upfront in year one.

Why Students Choose Nepal

  • Easy overland/short-flight travel from India
  • Similar syllabus and disease exposure to Indian training
  • Strong exam-performance track record

Things to Consider

  • Higher overall fees than most other destinations
  • A large first-year payment is typically required

Study MBBS in Georgia

Georgia has grown in popularity thanks to its safety record, pleasant climate (similar to parts of India), and rising English proficiency among the local population.

Why Students Choose Georgia

  • Strong safety rankings
  • Climate similar to India
  • Growing English-speaking population
  • Direct flights from Delhi (~5 hours)
  • Part-time work opportunities for students

Things to Consider

  • Higher overall budget than Russia, Uzbekistan or Kyrgyzstan
  • No university-run hostels in most cases — private accommodation is arranged instead

🇬🇪 MBBS in Georgia

Study MBBS in Georgia

A top pick for a European MBBS experience — strong FMGE outcomes, a climate similar to India's, rising English fluency, and one of the safer study-abroad destinations.

Overview

Georgia is a country in the Caucasus, at the crossroads of Eastern Europe and Western Asia, and is considered one of the top options for Indian students wanting a European MBBS experience. Indian graduates of Georgian medical universities have shown a higher FMGE pass rate than their counterparts from Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Belarus, China, and Uzbekistan.

NEET qualifying marks are required for admission, and students must also pass an entrance interview to demonstrate English proficiency. The climate is relatively similar to India's, the quality of education is considered strong, and Georgia is regarded as one of the safer study-abroad destinations — it was recently ranked the 4th-safest country in Europe at night.

Per the Ministry of Education of Georgia, the medical program (MD, equivalent to MBBS) runs 6 years, including a 6th-year internship. English is the medium of instruction, and English is spoken by more than half of Georgia's population. Georgia is more expensive than MBBS destinations such as Russia, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Bangladesh, China, Tajikistan, Timor-Leste, Mongolia, and Uzbekistan.

Eligibility for Indian Students

Academic Qualification

  • Completed classes 11 and 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB)
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB in class 12 (40% for reserved categories)

NEET

Mandatory per NMC guidelines to be eligible for admission with a valid student visa.

Studying Without NEET

⚠️ Universities can issue an admission letter without a NEET result, but the Embassy will not issue a visa without one — so in practice, an Indian passport holder cannot study MBBS in Georgia without qualifying NEET (a workaround reportedly exists — check directly with our counselors for current details).

Age Requirement

At least 17 years old by December 31 of the admission year.

English Proficiency

No IELTS/TOEFL required, but each university conducts an online interview to assess basic English proficiency.

Is MBBS in Georgia Valid in India?

Georgia's Ministry of Education, Science and Youth states that the country's MD/MBBS program (6 years, 360 ECTS, including a 6th-year internship) complies with NMC's FMGL Regulations. Key points from Georgia's official position (via the Embassy of Georgia to India):

  • Medical programs are accredited by Georgia's National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement, itself recognised by the World Federation of Medical Education (WFME)
  • Graduates are eligible to work as a "junior doctor" in Georgia without additional exams, performing duties under a certified specialist's guidance (or independently in emergencies if no certified doctor is available)
  • This is presented as equivalent to the MBBS qualification in India and the UK

⚠️ NMC has not published an official confirmed list of FMGL-compliant countries, so there is inherent regulatory uncertainty in any MBBS-abroad decision, including Georgia.

World Recognition

Georgian medical degrees are recognised by:

  • NMC (India) — per Georgia's Ministry of Education's own position
  • WHO (World Health Organization)
  • ECFMG (Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates, USA)
  • WFME (World Federation for Medical Education)
  • FAIMER (Foundation for Advancement of International Medical Education and Research)

Georgia's climate (pleasant summers, mild winters) is considered more similar to India's than Russia, Kyrgyzstan, or Kazakhstan's, which can make clinical adjustment easier since disease patterns in similar climates tend to overlap.

Local Language

  • English proficiency among Georgians has risen significantly over the past two decades due to government emphasis; English is mandatory in schools and Georgia has discussed making it an official language
  • Younger Georgians generally speak/understand English; local students often study MBBS in English too
  • Georgian language study is compulsory for international MBBS students for the first 3 years of the program
  • Older generations are more likely to know only Georgian

Safety

Georgia is frequently cited among the safer countries for international students; Tbilisi alone is home to over 20,000 Indian students and students from 50+ other countries. It was ranked the 4th-safest country in Europe for night walks.

⚠️ Context as of this content's 2025/2026 source: the Russia-Ukraine conflict has affected perceptions of Russia/Ukraine as study destinations, and recent unrest has affected perceptions of Bangladesh — verify current conditions independently before advising students.

Budget, Scholarships & Hostels

  • Average tuition: USD 5,000–6,000/year (~₹4.3–5.2 lakh/year)
  • Accommodation + food in Tbilisi: ~USD 300/month (~₹25,000/month)
  • Estimated total cost (tuition + living) for the full program: ~₹40–50 lakh, with some universities running as high as ₹55–60 lakh
  • For comparison, Romania, Serbia, and Bulgaria (EU) run ~₹55–70 lakh; students with a ~₹30 lakh budget are generally better suited to Russia, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, or Kazakhstan instead of Georgia
  • No scholarships are currently offered by Georgian medical universities
  • Universities in Georgia generally do not provide on-campus hostels — housing is typically arranged through admissions consultancies/agents rather than the university itself

Part-Time Work & Post-Graduation Path

Georgia is noted as one of the few popular MBBS-abroad destinations that allows part-time work for international students, with reported earnings of ₹25,000–₹1 lakh/month.

After MBBS/MD in Georgia, pursuing a US postgraduate residency generally involves:

  • Confirming ECFMG recognition
  • Passing USMLE Steps 1, 2 CK and 2 CS
  • ECFMG credential verification
  • Gaining US clinical experience (electives/observerships)
  • Applying through the NRMP residency match
  • Securing a J-1 or H-1B visa
  • Completing a 3–7 year residency (specialty-dependent)
  • Pursuing board certification afterward

Georgia vs. Other Countries — Quick Reference

  • Vs. Russia/Kyrgyzstan/Kazakhstan/Uzbekistan: Georgia has a notably higher FMGE pass rate across the years shown above, a more familiar climate, growing English fluency among locals, and part-time work options — but at a meaningfully higher cost (~₹40–60 lakh vs. ~₹22–37 lakh in Kazakhstan, for comparison)
  • Vs. EU countries (Romania, Serbia, Bulgaria): Georgia is generally more affordable
  • Direct flights: Delhi–Tbilisi is a direct ~5–5.5 hour flight, comparable in convenience to Delhi–Almaty (Kazakhstan)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Georgian MBBS degree valid in India?
Georgia's Ministry of Education states its MD/MBBS program complies with NMC's FMGL Regulations, but NMC itself hasn't published a confirmed list of compliant countries — clearing the FMGE/NExT licensing exam on return is what ultimately determines eligibility to practice in India.
What's the flight time and cost from Delhi?
A direct flight takes roughly 5–5.5 hours, with one-way tickets typically ₹15,000–₹20,000.
Are scholarships available?
No — Georgian medical universities generally don't offer scholarships; most students self-fund or take an education loan.
Can I study MBBS in Georgia without NEET?
Universities can issue an admission letter without a NEET result, but the Indian Embassy won't issue a student visa without one — so in practice, NEET is required.
What's the total budget for the full program?
Roughly ₹40–50 lakh including tuition and living costs, running as high as ₹55–60 lakh at some universities — ask our counselors for a breakdown for your shortlisted options.
